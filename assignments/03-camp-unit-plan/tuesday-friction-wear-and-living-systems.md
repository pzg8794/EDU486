## One Question

**After plastic wears into smaller pieces, how might living things encounter
it, and what evidence would we still need?**

Use one evidence phrase only when it becomes useful:

- **WE SAW:** something we directly noticed.
- **A SOURCE SUPPORTS:** information from a trusted source.
- **WE STILL NEED TO TEST:** something today's evidence cannot answer.

## Safety Language

**“We will not make or handle tire dust because breathing or touching it is not
needed for this investigation, and loose dust could contaminate our samples.
Intact wheels, safe touch after an adult check, measurements, photographs, and
sealed evidence let us investigate safely.”**

Youth do not sand, scrape, cut, heat, or intentionally create particles. Youth
do not handle KOH. A warm wheel shows energy transfer, not a measured particle
count. A filtered particle remains **suspected**, not confirmed plastic.
