## Safety Language

**“We will not make tire dust. We do not want to breathe it, touch it, or
contaminate our samples. Intact wheels, measurements, photographs, and sealed
evidence let us investigate safely.”**

Youth do not sand, scrape, cut, heat, or intentionally create particles. Youth
do not handle KOH. A warm wheel shows energy transfer, not a measured particle
count. A filtered particle remains **suspected**, not confirmed plastic.

## What Counts As Success

1. One before/after wheel observation.
2. One evidence-limited model revision.
3. Seven matched covered filters: **BLANK + SAMPLE 1-6**.
4. One youth-selected question or idea for Wednesday.
