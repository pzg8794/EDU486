## Prompt Ladder

Ask only one at a time:

1. **What do you notice?**
2. **Is that observed, possible, or still unknown?**
3. **Who makes or maintains that part of the system?**
4. **What fair change do you want?**

## Access And Evidence

- Offer microscope, magnifier, enlarged image, drawing, pointing, speaking,
  partner, seated/quiet, pass, and return routes before anyone has to ask.
- Both scholars' maps and ideas enter the shared record, but neither scholar
  must disclose a home address, health history, identity, or family experience.
- Compare infrastructure and methods, not neighborhoods or people as "better"
  or "worse."
- A visual particle remains **suspected**, not confirmed plastic. Tuesday's
  51+ count is preliminary until route distance and counting rules are checked.

## Success

1. One careful filter observation beside the blank.
2. One same-method place comparison.
3. One power/decision connection and one fair request.
4. One youth-chosen Thursday station with roles and a short rehearsal.
