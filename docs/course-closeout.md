This record separates a finished artifact from a verified external submission.
An authored file, Drive document, or reviewed visual is **artifact complete**
when it is ready for its intended audience. It is **submission verified** only
when Blackboard, Sent mail, or another direct delivery record confirms that the
intended recipient received it. A missing receipt does not prove that work was
not submitted, but it must not be represented as confirmation.

## Poster And Youth-Media Boundary

The final Plastic Pirates poster uses actual youth photographs and artifacts.
It is preserved in protected local submission storage and is intentionally
excluded from this public repository. The public record may document the
research question, methods, findings, evidence limits, production dimensions,
and delivery status, but it must not publish the youth-photo poster or treat an
AI-assisted communication image as a replacement for the original evidence.

Raw photographs, videos, unreviewed automatic transcripts, contact details,
incidental personal disclosures, and private submission receipts remain under
`_local-course-materials/` or another access-controlled location. Only reviewed,
deidentified records belong in public Git.
