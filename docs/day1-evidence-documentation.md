## Access-To-Agency Findings

**Access routes observed:** looking, walking, choosing, scooping or screening,
holding a jar, labeling, writing, counting, using tweezers, using a lighted
magnifier, reading, listening, explaining, and questioning. Seated and standing
routes were visible.

**Design limitations:** the full participation menu was not consistently
visible; oral closure received the clearest camera attention; timed work could
still make speed more visible than evidence; and a lab-coat comment briefly
tied science identity to clothing before being corrected.

**Monday redesign:** recognize scientific identity through noticing,
questioning, comparing, testing, revising, caring for evidence, and helping the
group think. Offer speaking, drawing, pointing, writing, dictation, tool-use,
partner, seated, private-audio, and pass-and-return routes to the same rigorous
goal.

## Science Corrections

**The prepared jars supported a model comparison; they did not confirm
microplastics in a field sample or prove a source-to-body pathway.**

- Call seeded contents **prepared model pieces**.
- Call visually observed field material **suspected particles** until an
  appropriate method confirms material identity.
- State that magnification changed what participants could notice.
- Separate detection, possible route, exposure, dose, and health effect.
- Label claims **SUPPORTED**, **POSSIBLE**, or **STILL UNKNOWN**.
- Replace fear-based certainty with calm evidence limits.

Direct checks:

- [EPA: Microplastics Research](https://www.epa.gov/water-research/microplastics-research)
- [FDA: Microplastics and Nanoplastics in Foods](https://www.fda.gov/food/environmental-contaminants-food/microplastics-and-nanoplastics-foods)
- [NOAA repository: visual marine-debris identification limits](https://repository.library.noaa.gov/view/noaa/57266)
- [NOAA Marine Debris Program: prevention](https://marinedebris.noaa.gov/our-work/prevention)
- [NOAA Marine Debris Program: removal](https://marinedebris.noaa.gov/our-work/removal)

## Privacy And Interpretation Boundary

- No facial recognition or child identification was performed.
- No diagnosis, disability, race, ethnicity, health status, emotion, family
  circumstance, motivation, or private identity was inferred.
- No synthetic images of youth were generated.
- All selected visual evidence uses real source frames and remains private
  until Piter completes consent and context review.
- Absence from a recording is an evidence gap, not proof that participation,
  access, learning, or joy was absent.
- Automatic transcript wording and speaker separation remain fallible; every
  public quotation requires source-audio review.
