## Day 4 Learning Story

The Day 4 evidence follows the responsive sequence that actually occurred:
model review and revision, a mayor/policy role-play, arguments and
counterarguments about responsibility, and wearable art for advocacy. The
record preserves multiple participation routes and separates a youth-created
claim from proof that a proposed policy will produce a particular outcome.

## Day 5 Learning Story

The Day 5 evidence follows the youth-led showcase: practice, connection across
the week, teaching with the trifold and artifacts, advocacy, and recognition.
The two youth souvenirs foreground different contributions: making a public
care message and using measurement to make community evidence count. The
reviewed visuals support memory and explanation; the original photographs
remain the primary record.

## Evidence Limits

- Photographs show visible participation and artifacts; they do not prove what
  a learner understood, felt, or chose privately.
- `51+` is a record of visible litter observations across an approximately
  406-meter route, not a count of chemically confirmed plastic particles.
- Sand, fish, filtration, air, and tissue investigations were teaching models
  and observations. They do not establish a complete human exposure route or
  health effect.
- AI-assisted images clarify labels, arrows, contrast, and activity purpose.
  They are communication artifacts, not replacements for original evidence.
