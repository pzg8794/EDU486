# July 24 Camp Day 1 Video Transcripts

This dated folder is part of the EDU486 repository's existing
`transcripts/` library. It contains 18 de-identified video transcripts and
one chronological master record.

## Source Hierarchy

1. **Friday video transcripts:** contemporaneous evidence of what was audible.
2. **July 24 plus/arrow reflection:** facilitator recollection and next-step
   commitments after camp.
3. **July 21 planning record:** intended activity, access routes, materials,
   and evidence rules before camp.
4. **July 15-16 planning records:** earlier storyline and lesson decisions.

Planning establishes intention; video establishes recorded activity; reflection
establishes later interpretation. The three are not interchangeable.

## Quality Boundary

- Every quotation requires checking against source audio.
- `Speaker unclear` is intentional because reliable local diarization was not
  available.
- Video 18 is explicitly unusable for speech evidence because the automatic
  transcript became systematically repetitive.
- No visible silence, movement, speech rate, or media polish is treated as proof
  of engagement, understanding, motivation, diagnosis, or identity.
- The source videos, token-level outputs, and incidental private conversation
  remain in the private evidence vault.
