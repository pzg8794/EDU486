## What Felt Strong

**Piter made it to the first lesson and was able to join the work despite the
transportation problem.** He described Aastha as fully engaged, excited, and
receptive, which mattered because the team had to begin teaching while also
adjusting to the actual setting.

**Youth asked questions and entered the investigation without needing every
question to be supplied by the adults.** Piter valued that the participation
felt natural and connected to what youth were noticing. The video record must
still be used to identify the specific questions, observations, and model moves.

**The team had more activities available than could fit in the station.** That
is useful preparation, but only if the team treats those activities as options
and chooses the right move for the moment instead of trying to complete every
page of the plan.

## What Must Change

**Use what youth showed us to make Monday more personal, engaging, and
meaningful.** Piter's main arrow was not simply to repeat Friday more smoothly.
It was to revise the next lesson around youth observations, questions,
participation routes, and the kinds of support each learner actually used.

**Ask more and tell less.** The instructors named a pattern that also appears in
the video evidence: facilitators sometimes explained the conclusion instead of
leaving enough space for youth to build it. On Monday, prompts should begin with
what youth notice, what evidence supports an idea, and what they still need to
find out.

**Protect time without rushing thinking.** The team needs a visible timer,
shorter directions, a clear must-do sequence, and a named activity that can be
skipped. Running out of time should not eliminate the closing question or the
chance for youth to record what changed in their thinking.

**Prepare together before youth arrive.** Piter and Aastha need to review the
lesson, test the materials, and agree on who launches, who watches time, who
records youth questions, and who offers proactive support. Piter said he could
transport the Monday materials after the team organized them over the weekend.
