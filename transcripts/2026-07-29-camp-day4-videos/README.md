This dated folder is part of EDU486's existing `transcripts/` library. It
contains 4 de-identified Wednesday video transcripts and one

## Source Hierarchy

1. **Wednesday video transcripts:** contemporaneous evidence of what was audible.
2. **Wednesday source photos and real video frames:** contemporaneous visual evidence.
3. **July 29 lecture, planning, and debrief transcripts:** context, later interpretation,
   scientific cautions, and next-step commitments.
4. **Wednesday lesson plan:** intended sequence, access routes, and evidence goals.

Planning establishes intention; media establishes what was recorded; debrief
establishes later interpretation. They are not interchangeable.

## Quality Boundary

- Every quotation requires checking against source audio.
- `Speaker unclear` is intentional because reliable local diarization was unavailable.
- No visible silence, movement, speech rate, or media polish is treated as
  proof of engagement, understanding, motivation, diagnosis, or identity.
- Raw footage, token-level output, and incidental private conversation remain
  in the private evidence vault.
