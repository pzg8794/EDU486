## 2. Evidence Jars: Predict, Count, Magnify, Compare

**Approximate time:** 11:34-11:50 a.m.
**Activity:** Youth predicted a count, searched visually, counted with tools,
used magnification, and compared the result with their first estimate.

**Facilitator, 11:36 video at 03:38**
> Scientists found several sick birds near Lake Ontario. To understand what
> might have happened, they need to know which beach the birds came from. The
> only clue is sand collected from around where each bird was found. Examine
> the evidence jars, observe the sand, predict how many pieces of plastic you
> think are inside, search and count, use a magnifying glass, record your
> evidence, and decide where the birds most likely came from.
