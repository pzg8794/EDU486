## Boundary

Only camp evidence recorded between **7:15 a.m. and 1:00 p.m. EDT** is
included. A day may begin later in this record when no usable recording exists
for the earlier part of the session.

The clean edition excludes:

- Friday clips recorded after 1:00 p.m.;
- post-camp planning, staff debriefs, and equipment closeout;
- repeated instructions, setup noise, and speech too unclear to recover;
- youth names, incidental personal disclosures, and contact information;
- unrelated financial-hardship recordings that were mislabeled as camp files;
- claims that a suspected particle was chemically confirmed as plastic; and
- claims that exposure alone proves a particular route or health effect.

Raw source recordings and automatic transcripts remain unchanged in local
evidence storage. They are not duplicated here.

## Speaker Labels

- **Youth A / Youth B:** used only when two distinct youth contributions can be
  separated within the same recorded activity. The letters do not identify a
  child by name and are not assumed to follow the same person across days.
- **Youth voice:** a youth contribution is audible, but the individual speaker
  cannot be assigned reliably.
- **Youth group:** several youth speak together or overlap.
- **Facilitator:** Piter, Aastha, or another camp facilitator when the role is
  clear but the individual voice is not safely assigned.
- **Instructor / guest scientist:** a course instructor or visiting scientist
  speaking in that role.

The source recordings did not support reliable diarization. These labels
organize participation without pretending to know more than the evidence
shows.

## Editing Method

The clean transcripts preserve the speaker's meaning and wording while:

1. removing filler, false starts, and repeated words when they add no meaning;
2. correcting punctuation and obvious automatic-transcription errors;
3. replacing uncertain speech with `[unclear]`;
4. placing each excerpt under the activity it documents; and
5. adding short evidence notes when a spoken claim exceeds what the activity
   established.

No youth statement was rewritten into formal academic language.
