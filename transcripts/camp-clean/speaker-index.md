## Why the Labels Are Anonymous

The source audio did not include reliable speaker diarization. This index
therefore organizes contributions by speaker role without publishing youth
names or pretending that the same anonymous letter follows the same child
across multiple days.

For a private human review, Piter or Aastha may compare the source video with
the timestamps. The public GitHub record must remain anonymized.
